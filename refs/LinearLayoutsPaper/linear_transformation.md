In general linear algebra, for a function $L$ to be considered a "linear transformation," it must satisfy two strict conditions:

1. **Additivity:** $L(A + B) = L(A) + L(B)$ (Which, in GF(2), is our XOR rule: $L(A \oplus B) = L(A) \oplus L(B)$).
2. **Scalar Homogeneity:** $L(c \cdot v) = c \cdot L(v)$, where $c$ is a scalar and $v$ is a vector.
